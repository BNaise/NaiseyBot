# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot ("the Bot", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

## 1. Data We Collect

The Bot does **not** collect, store, or log any personal data, message content, or user information. Specifically:

- We do not store message content sent to or received from the Bot.
- We do not log user IDs, usernames, or server IDs.
- We do not track command usage history.
- We do not use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking tools.

## 2. How the Bot Works

When you use a command, the Bot processes your request in real time (for example, reading the parameters you provide, such as a mentioned user) purely to generate and send its response. Once the response is sent, nothing about that interaction is retained.

## 3. Data Shared with Third Parties

Because the Bot does not collect or store data, there is no data to share with third parties. The Bot operates on Discord's platform and is subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy for any data Discord itself collects (which is outside our control).

## 4. Data Retention

Not applicable — the Bot retains no data after processing a command.

## 5. Your Rights

Since no personal data is stored, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. If this ever changes (for example, if future features require storing data), this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect that, along with instructions for exercising your data rights.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Bot does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, as it does not collect data at all. Use of the Bot is subject to Discord's own age requirements.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the Bot's functionality changes to include data storage. Continued use of the Bot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via Discord: https://www.discord.gg/pWRN9cVD7p
